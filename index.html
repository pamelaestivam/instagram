Follower Analyzer

Find who doesn't follow you back on Instagram — @estivampam. Your data never leaves this browser.

How to get your data (2 minutes, no password needed)
  1. Instagram app → Settings and activity → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.
  2. Create export → your Instagram account → Export to device.
  3. Under Customize information pick only Followers and following. Set date range All time and format JSON.
  4. Wait for the email, download the ZIP and unzip it. The files are in connections/followers_and_following/.
